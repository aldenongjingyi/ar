AR Demo Setup Guide

1 Generate Your AR QR Code

This is your AR marker — print it or display it on screen.

2 Compile the AR Target (.mind file)

  • 1 Download your QR code from Step 1 above (save it as qr-target.png)
  • 2 Open the MindAR compiler tool: hiukim.github.io/mind-ar-js-doc/tools/compile
  • 3 Upload your qr-target.png and click Start
  • 4 When it finishes, click Export to download targets.mind
  • 5 Place targets.mind in the same folder as index.html

3 Project File Structure

Your folder should look like this:

# AR HTML/
├── index.html # AR experience
├── targets.mind # compiled from your QR
├── qr-target.png # QR code image
└── setup.html # this file

4 Host & Test

  • 1 Host the files on a server with HTTPS (required for camera access). Options: Vercel, Netlify, GitHub Pages, or any web host.
  • 2 Open index.html on your iPhone or Android device in the browser.
  • 3 Tap Launch AR, allow camera access, then point the camera at the printed or displayed QR code.
  • 4 The 3D MAP72 logo will appear floating above the QR code.
Local testing: Use npx serve . or python3 -m http.server 8080 and tunnel it with ngrok to get an HTTPS URL for mobile testing.
